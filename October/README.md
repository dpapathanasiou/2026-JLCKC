# 1, 2 [シルエット](<./2026 OCTOBER 1-2.pdf>)

> silhouette

- 影絵。
    > A shadow picture.
- また、全体の形、輪郭。
    > A full-body outline or contour.
- 十八世紀のフランスの蔵相シルエットの名ぬ由来。
    > The term originates from the name of the Finance Minister of France in the 18th century.
- シルエットが節約のため、輪郭の中を黒く塗っただけの単色の肖像画を推奨したからとも、黒紙の切り絵を好んだからともいわれる。
    > Silhouette recommended monochrome portraits, with only the outline filled in with black, in order to save money; he preferred cutouts on black paper to regular portraits.

# 3, 4 [一張羅](<./2026 OCTOBER 3-4.pdf>)

###  いっちょうら 

> one's Sunday best​  
> one's only good clothes; one's best clothes

- たった一枚しかない晴れ着。
    > It refers to one's single best outfit.
- また、着替えのない、たった一枚の着物。
    > The only fine garment one owns, with no other to change into.
- 「一張」は、数え方の単位で、「羅」は、薄絹のこと。
    > The first part of the expression is a counter for such garments, while the final character refers to a type of sheer silk.
- 一張羅が変化して、「一張来」ともいう。
    > The expression evolved from this combination of terms, and it is sometimes also written with a different final character.

# 5, 6, 7 [獅子身中の虫](<./2026 OCTOBER 5-7.pdf>)

### しししんちゅうのむし

> snake in one's bosom; treacherous friend​  
> thorn in one's side

- 内部から災いを起こす人。
    > A person who causes disaster from within.
- 恩を仇で返す人。
    > Someone who repays kindness with ingratitude.
- 「梵網経」の下巻にあることば。
    >  This term appears in the second volume of the "*Brahmajala Sutra,*" also known as the Sutra of Brahma's Net.
- 獅子の体内に寄生している虫が、その獅子の肉を食い、やがて獅子を滅ぼすということから。
    > The expression is based on the parasites living inside lions,  devouring their flesh, and eventually destroying them from within.
- 仏教では、仏教徒でありながら、仏教に害をあたえる者のたとえとして使われる。
    > In Buddhism, it is used as a metaphor for someone who causes harm to the faith, despite professing to be a Buddhist.

# 8, 9 [栞](<./2026 OCTOBER 8-9.pdf>)

### しおり

> bookmark  
> guidebook

- 書物の読みかけのところにはさんで目印とする紙片やひも。
    > A piece of paper or string used as a marker to keep track of a place in a book.
- もとは「枝折り」。
    > It can also be written this way.
- 山道を行くとき、木の枝を折って帰りの目印とすることをいった。
    > The phrase originates from the practice of breaking off a branch to use as a landmark when walking along a mountain path.
- それが転じて、書物の間にはさんで目印とするものや、案内書などを「しおり」というようになった。
    > This meaning evolved to refer to something placed between the pages of a book to mark a point; it also means guidebook more generally.

# 10, 11 [筈](<2026 OCTOBER 10-11.pdf>)

### はず

> should (be); bound (to be); expected (to be); must (be)

- （物事が）当然そうなること。
    > Things which happen naturally.
- 「筈」は、「矢筈」の意で、矢を弓につがえるとき、弓の弦からはずれないように矢の末端につけるものを指す。
    > The term comes from one of the characters for "*arrow holder,*" referring to the part that is attached to the tip of an arrow, to prevent it from coming off the string of the bow when it is strung on a bow.
- その矢筈と弦は、当然うまく合うようにできていることから、「当然そうなること」の意が生じた。
    > The arrowhead and string were naturally designed to fit well together, and so the first character came to imply one followed from the other,  of course, which took on a more abstract meaning.

# 12, 13, 14 [一頭地を抜く](<./2026 OCTOBER 12-14.pdf>)

### いっとうちをぬく

> to cut a conspicuous figure  
> to be the best by far 

- 人より一歩抜きんでる。
    > To stand a cut above the others.
- 「一頭地」の「地」は、助辞で、意味はなく「一頭」と同じ意。
    > The final character in the first part of the phrase is a particle, which carries no meaning of its own.
- 他の者より頭一つ分だけ抜け出ている意から。
    > This expression effectively means "*one head*," that is, the image of being a full head height above everyone else.

# 15, 16 [三文判](<./2026 OCTOBER 15-16.pdf>)

### さんもんばん

> ready-made seal; cheap seal​

- できあいの安い印鑑。
    > An inexpensive, ready-made personal seal.
- 「三文」は、一文銭三枚分の値。
    > The first two characters refer to the value of three one-mon coins, the mon being the currency of Japan during the Muromachi period.
- 転じて、非常に安価なこと。
    > By extension, it signifies something very cheap. 
- 「判」は、印判の意。
    > The final character means seal or stamp.

# 17, 18 [軍門に降る](<./2026 OCTOBER 17-18.pdf>)

### ぐんもんにくだる

> to capitulate; to surrender; to submit to; to concede​

- 戦争や試合に負けたり、競争に敗れたりして、相手に屈服するたとえ。
    > A metaphor for submitting to an opponent after being defeated in a war, a match, or a competition.
- 「軍門」は、戦場における陣営の出入り口のこと。
    > The first two characters literally mean "*military gate*" and refer to the entrance of a military camp on a battlefield.
- 投降して敵の軍門に入るという意から。
    > The expression originates from the act of surrendering and entering the enemy's camp.

# 19, 20, 21 [堪能](<./2026 OCTOBER 19-21.pdf>)

### たんのう

> proficient; skilled; skillful; good; accomplished  
> enjoying to the full; full satisfaction; being satiated (with); having one's fill (of)​

- 十分に味わい、満足すること。
    > To fully enjoy or experience something to one's satisfaction.
- もとは、「十分に足りる」という意の「足んぬ」。
    > The term originated from the word *tannu*, meaning "to be fully sufficient."
- それが「たんぬ」→「たんの」→「たんのう」と変化したもの。
    > Over time, it evolved from *tannu* to *tanno* and finally *tannō*.
- 「堪能」は、当て字。
    > The kanji representation is an example of *ateji,* which is the selection of characters for their sound, rather than their meanings.

# 22, 23 [肝心](<./2026 OCTOBER 22-23.pdf>)

### かんじん

> essential; important; crucial; vital; main​

- とくに大切であるさま。
    > Something which is especially important.
- もとは「かんしん」と読み、肝臓と心臓のこと。
    > Originally pronounced "*kanshin,*" it refers to the liver and heart.
- 漢方でいう五臓（心臓肝臓腎臓脾臓肺臓）のうち、肝臓と心臓がとくに重要な臓器であることから。
    > In Chinese medicine, the liver and heart are the most important of the "five organs" (heart, liver, kidneys, spleen, and lungs).
- 「肝腎」とも書く。
    > It can also be written using the characters for liver and kidney.

# 24, 25 [狼狽](<./2026 OCTOBER 24-25.pdf>)

### ろうばい

> confusion; dismay; consternation; panic​

- うろえること。
    > A state of confusion or panic.
- 「狼」も「狽」も、中国の伝説上の動物。
    > Both characters are types of wolves, legendary animals from Chinese folklore.
- 狼は後ろ足が短く、狽は前足が短いため、狽が狼の背に乗り、常に一組になって歩くという。
    > It is said that one has short hind legs, while the other has short forelegs; consequently, the latter rides on the back of the former, and so the two always move together as a pair.
- 両者が離れると、うまく歩けなくなって慌ててしまうことから。
    > If they become separated, they are unable to walk properly and fall into a state of panicked confusion, a phenomenon from which the term is derived.

# 26, 27, 28 [加薬](<./2026 OCTOBER 26-28.pdf>)

### かやく

> spices; condiments; seasoning

- サンショウ、ショウガ、ネギなど料理に添える香辛料。薬味。
    > Condiments or seasonings, such as Japanese pepper, ginger, or green onions, which are added to dishes.
- また、五目飯、うどんなどに入れる具。
    > The term also refers to ingredients added to mixed rice or udon noodles.
- 漢方の言葉で、主要薬の効果を高めるための補助薬のこと。
    > In traditional Chinese medicine, it denotes an auxiliary agent used to enhance the efficacy of a primary medicinal substance.
- 転じて、料理に加える薬味や、飯や麺類に加えるさまざま具の意になった。
    > By extension, the term has come to refer to seasonings added to food, as well as the various ingredients incorporated into rice or noodle dishes.

# 29, 30 [生兵法](<./2026 OCTOBER 29-30.pdf>)

### なまびょうほう

> crude tactics; smattering of knowledge​

- 中途半端な知識や技能。
    > Refers to incomplete or superficial knowledge and skills.
- 「生兵法は大怪我のもと（中途半端な知識や技能で事を行うと大失敗する）」などと使われる。
    > It is often used in the expression, "A little knowledge is a dangerous thing" (literally: "A half-baked mastery of martial arts leads to serious injury," meaning that attempting something with only partial knowledge or skill can result in a major failure).
- 「兵法」は、武術の心得。
    > The last two characters mean knowledge of martial arts.
- 「生」は、中途半端であることと示す接統語。
    > While the prefix character means "raw," indicating incompleteness.
- つまり、武術の心得はあっても、非常に未熟であるという意。
    > In other words, it describes a state where one possesses some knowledge of martial arts but remains highly unskilled.
- 転じて、知識や技術などまだ身についてないことをいう言葉になった。
    > Over time, the term has come to be used more broadly to describe a lack of proper mastery over any form of knowledge or skill.

# 31, 1 [November](<../November/README.md>) [超弩級](<../November/2026 OCTOBER - 31 NOVEMBER 1.pdf>)

### ちょうきゅう

> superlative; extraordinary; mega; outstanding​  
> super-dreadnought (class of warships)

- けた外れであること。巨大な等級。
    > Denoting something of extraordinary scale or class.
- 超弩級の「弩」は、一九〇六年につくられたイギリスの戦艦「ドレッドノート」の頭の文字に漢字を当てたもの。
    > The middle character is a kanji representation derived from the name of the British battleship "*Dreadnought*," built in 1906.
- この戦艦は、画期的なほど巨大だったので、巨大な等級を示す「弩級」、その上の「超弩級」と言う言葉が生まれた。
    > That battleship was so groundbreakingly enormous that it gave rise to the term "dreadnought class," signifying a new breed of vessels, which evolved into this expression.
