# 1, 2 [相棒](<./2026 AUGUST 1-2.pdf>)

### あいぼう

> partner; pal; accomplice​

- 一緒に物事を行う相手。
    > Someone you do things with.
- もとは、駕龍などを一緒にかつぐ相手のことをいった。
    > Originally, it referred to the two people who carried a litter (also known as a sedan chair, palanquin, or dooly, a type of wheel-less vehicles for the transport of people) together.
- 駕龍の上部に一本の棒を通して、その前後を二人でかついだことから。
    > The phrase refers to the single rod or stick which passes all the way through the top of the litter, which the two people held on to, front and back.
