# 3, 4 [逐電](<./2026 SEPTEMBER 3-4.pdf>)

### ちくでん

> flight; absconding​

- 行方をくらますこと。
    > Disappearing without a trace.
- 逃亡。
    > Fleeing.
- 「逐」は追う、「電」は稲妻の意。
    > The first character means "to chase," and second one means "lightning." 
- 元来は稲妻を追いかける意で、行動の速いさまをたとえていう語。
    > Originally referring to the act of chasing after lightning, the term is used metaphorically, to describe swift action.


# 5, 6 [市松模様](<./2026 SEPTEMBER 5-6.pdf>)

### いちまつもよう 

> checked/checkered/checkerboard pattern; checks; checkers

- 色違いの四角形を交互に並べた、碁盤目状の模様。
    > A grid-like pattern of alternating colored squares.
- 江戸時代の役者、初代佐野川市松がこの模様の袴をつけていたことから。
    > It is named after Ichimatsu Sanogawa, a kabuki actor of the Edo period, who wore *hakama* (loose-legged trousers worn over a kimono) featuring this design.

# 7, 8, 9 [案山子](<./2026 SEPTEMBER 7-9.pdf>)

### かかし

> scarecrow  
> figurehead; dummy

- 作物を荒らす鳥獣ををおどすために田畑に立てる人形のこと。
    > A doll that is erected in a field to scare off birds and beasts that destroy crops.
- 「嗅がし」からとする説が有力。
    > The most popular theory of why it works is because of the smell.
- 獣肉・毛髪を焼いたものなど、鳥獣が嫌うにおいを田畑に置いたことから。
    > The scarecrows placed in the fields were made of burnt animal meat and hair, odors that birds and animals dislike.

# 10, 11 [檄を飛ばす](<./2026 SEPTEMBER 10-11.pdf>)

### げきをとばす

> to make an appeal to the public; to rally people to one's cause; to make call to action​  
> to spur on; to fire someone up; to rally troops, employees, et. al.; to give a pep talk to; to shout encouragement​

- 自分の考えを求める。
    > Asking for one's thoughts.
- また、人々に行動を促す。
    > It also means inspiring people to take action.
- 「飛檄」を訓読したもの。
    > The expression derives from the *kun yomi*, or the native Japanese reading of Chinese characters, for the combination of "fly" and "manifesto."
- 「檄」は、「檄文」ともいい、昔の中国で、民衆に呼びかけるために役所が木札に書いて出した文書のこと。
    > The first character, which is also written together with the character for writing and literature, refers to a document written on wooden tags by government offices in ancient China, to appeal to the people.
- 「檄を飛ばす」は、その文書を急いで回す意。
    > The full expression means passing the document around quickly, with a sense of urgency.
